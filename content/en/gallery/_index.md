## UVU Architecture Renderings

We were honored to have the Rio Grande Plan featured as a subject for UVU Architecture! Students created plans and renderings of a fully-realized Rio Grande Depot serving as Salt Lake City's central transit station. Our sincere thanks to these students and professors/mentors for all these beautifully crafted renderings!
